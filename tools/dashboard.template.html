Scenario drift

One column per deep session; segments are that session's scenarios, stacked to 100%. A thin ribbon connects two segments only when both declare the same scenario family in adjacent deep sessions — no shared family means no ribbon, and no claimed continuity. Hollow markers are backfilled sessions — values reconstructed from prose, read with care.

Macro series
